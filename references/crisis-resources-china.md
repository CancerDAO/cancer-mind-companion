# Crisis Resources in China: Mental Health and Suicide Prevention Hotlines

## National Mental Health Resources

### 12356 — National Unified Psychological Aid Hotline
- **Available:** 24 hours
- **Notes:** Established by the National Health Commission (国家卫生健康委) as the unified national psychological aid hotline. Fully operational as of May 1, 2025 per government directive. Each city must have at least 1 hotline with minimum 2 seats, providing at least 18 hours/day of service.

**Source:** Government of China — National Health Commission Notice (Gov.cn); https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6994470.htm

## Emergency Services

### For Immediate Danger
In China, when someone is actively suicidal or in a psychiatric emergency:

1. **Call 110** (Police) — for imminent danger,救人永远是第一位的
2. **Call 120** (Ambulance/Medical Emergency)
3. **Call 119** (Fire/Rescue) — can respond to psychiatric emergencies

### Note on Hospital Emergency Departments
For psychiatric emergencies, patients can also be taken directly to:
- **Beijing Huilongguan Hospital** — psychiatric emergency services
- **Beijing Anding Hospital** — psychiatric emergency services
- **Shanghai Mental Health Center** (宛平南路600号)
- **Peking University Sixth Hospital** — psychiatric emergency (010-82801984)

## Using Crisis Hotlines: Guidance for Cancer Patients and Caregivers

### When to Call
- When having thoughts of suicide or self-harm
- When feeling unable to cope with emotional distress
- After a cancer diagnosis or during treatment crises
- When experiencing severe anxiety or panic
- When a patient or caregiver feels completely overwhelmed and has no one else to turn to

### What to Expect
- All hotlines provide confidential support
- Trained counselors will listen without judgment
- No fees beyond standard phone charges (hotlines are free or low-cost)
- Services available for both the patient and family members

### Notes for Cancer Caregivers
- Caregivers can also call on behalf of the patient if the patient is unwilling
- If the patient is actively suicidal, do not leave them alone — call 110 and stay with them until help arrives
- It is appropriate to contact multiple hotlines if one is busy
